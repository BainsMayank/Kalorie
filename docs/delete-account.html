Delete your account

You only have a Kalorie account if you chose to sign in. Without one, everything is on your phone and uninstalling the app removes it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kalorie and go to Profile → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Tap Delete my account to confirm.

It happens at once.

Without the app

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Kalorie account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to say it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its email address.
  • Your place in a group, the name you used there, and the foods you shared with it.
  • Anything else kept online for your account.

Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from routine backups made by our hosting provider (Supabase), which are overwritten in their normal cycle. What is on your phone stays until you uninstall the app.
