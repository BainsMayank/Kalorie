Kalorie

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Kalorie is a free calorie and nutrition tracker with no ads. This is what it keeps about you, in plain words.

On your phone

Everything you log — food, goals, weight, water, recipes, thalis and label photos — is saved on your phone. You don't need an account for any of it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, we keep your email address, so we can send you a sign-in code. Nothing else leaves your phone, except foods you choose to share with a group. If backup is added in a later update, a copy of your log will be kept with your account too — this page and the app will say so first.

Where it is kept

Accounts are kept by Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. Each account can open only its own data, and a group's foods can be seen only by the people in that group: the database itself checks this on every request. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

If you join a group

The people in your group see the group's name, the name you give yourself in it, and the foods you choose to share: name, brand, barcode, nutrients and portion sizes. Your log, your weight, your goals and your label photos are never shared. When you leave, the foods you shared leave with you.

Never sold

Your data is never sold, never shared with advertisers and never used for ads. There are no ads, and no tracking or analytics tools inside the app.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a packet, only its barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product. Nothing about you goes with it.

Camera, photos and reminders

The camera is used only to read barcodes and to photograph nutrition labels, and photos are opened only when you pick one. Label photos stay on your phone. Reminders are set on your phone itself; no server sends them.

Deleting

In the app, Profile → Account → Delete my account and data removes your account and everything kept online for it, at once. To remove what is on your phone, uninstall the app. No longer have the app? See how to delete your account.

Children

Kalorie is not made for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use Kalorie without signing in (everything then stays on your phone), or sign in only with a parent's or guardian's OK.

Changes and contact

If what Kalorie keeps changes, this page and the app's Privacy screen will be updated first. Questions: CONTACT_EMAIL.
