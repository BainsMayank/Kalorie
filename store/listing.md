# Play Store listing

Copy each block into Play Console → *Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing*.
English (India) is the default language; add Hindi under *Manage translations → Add your own
translations → Hindi*. Limits: name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4,000
(the counts below were checked when this was written).

Graphics in this folder (made by `python3 scripts/app-icon/make_icons.py`):

- `icon-512.png` — App icon, 512 × 512
- `feature-graphic.png` — Feature graphic, 1024 × 500

Google doesn't allow prices, "free", rankings or "download now" in the icon, feature graphic or
screenshots, so those say none of that. The descriptions may mention that Kalorie is free.

Before going public, keep the description in line with the app: if a feature changes, change it
here too. The wording follows SPEC §7 (no guilt words, no "over").

### Full description (2,243 / 4,000)

```
Kalorie is a calorie and nutrition tracker made for the way India eats: dal, roti, sabzi, idli, biryani. Log in katoris, rotis and glasses, not only grams.

It's free, with no ads and no subscriptions, and you don't need an account. What you log stays on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Search more than 1,500 Indian dishes and ingredients, plus about 7,000 foods from around the world. "Daal", "dal" and "bhindi" all work, and small spelling slips are fine.
• Log a katori, roti, piece, cup or glass. Kalorie works out the grams.
• Less, normal or more oil and ghee: one tap changes the calories and fats.
• Scan the barcode on a food packet, or type in the label if it isn't found. Packets you've scanned before work offline.
• Save your own recipes and thalis, and log a whole meal in one go.
• See calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre for the day, and vitamins and minerals compared with the ICMR-NIN 2020 needs for Indians.
• Optional daily limits for sodium, sugar and saturated fat, with a calm note when you reach one.
• Track water and weight. A smooth weight trend shows the direction, not the day-to-day jumps.
• A calendar, and weekly and monthly trends.
• Gentle meal-time reminders, if you want them.

MADE TO BE KIND
• No guilt words and no red numbers. Eating more than planned shows in soft blue.
• Hide numbers: see shapes and words instead of calories and kilos.
• Just track: log without a calorie target at all.
• English and हिन्दी, light and dark mode. Works with large text and screen readers.

YOUR DATA
• Your log, weight and goals stay on your phone. No ads, no trackers, nothing sold.
• Export everything as spreadsheet files any time.
• An account is optional: sign in with a code sent to your email to start a family group and share your recipes and scanned packets. You can delete the account and its data from the app.

Food data: Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB, Anuvaad Solutions); Indian Food Composition Tables 2017 (© ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition); USDA FoodData Central; Open Food Facts (ODbL).

Kalorie helps you keep track of what you eat. It isn't medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. For a health condition, pregnancy or a special diet, please talk to a doctor or dietitian.
```
